## Brand & Style

The design system embodies **Calm Editorial Intelligence**—a disciplined, focused design language built for an Indonesian-first AI writing and text analysis workspace. Unlike typical SaaS and AI platforms that rely on saturated violet-blue gradients, faux-glassmorphism, and neon accents, this system grounds itself in editorial craftsmanship, structural honesty, and epistemic modesty.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Measured & Scholarly:** The interface prioritizes deep reading, side-by-side editing, and granular sentence-level critique. It behaves like high-grade editorial software rather than an automated novelty.
- **Trustworthy & Restrained:** Avoiding vanity percentages or hyperbolic detection guarantees, visual treatments reflect confidence through high typographic precision, quiet slate-tinted surfaces, and deliberate contrast.
- **Controlled & Intentional:** The user always feels in command of their prose. Layouts emphasize stability, spacious margins, clear spatial boundaries, and non-distracting functional chrome.

### Visual Grammar
A synthesis of **Modern Editorial Minimalism** and **Systematic Workspace Utility**:
- Crisp 1px structural borders defining workspaces and tool panels without visual noise.
- Pure white workspaces set against grounded mineral neutrals (`#F7F8F7` and `#EEF2F0`).
- No ambient colored glows, no backdrop-filter glass artifacts, and no decorative 3D flourishes.
- Status indications pair chromatic precision with unambiguous textual labels and iconography.

## Colors

The palette balances a deep botanical teal identity with neutral mineral tones, avoiding vibrant synthetic spectrums to foster extended cognitive focus during long writing sessions.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0F766E`)**: Anchors critical interactive elements, focused input highlights, primary workspace triggers, and verified badges.
- **Primary Dark (`#134E4A`)**: Used for high-emphasis interactive states (hover/active on primary CTAs), dense headers, and selected navigation states.
- **Primary Light (`#DDF3EF`)**: Soft tint for active item highlights, selected panel borders, subtle badge backdrops, and sentence-level emphasis in editorial modes.
- **Neutral / Text Primary (`#17231F`)**: Deepest forest-ink tone ensuring pristine typographic contrast (exceeding WCAG 2.2 AAA on white surfaces) without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F8F7`)**: Quiet, low-glare backdrop that frames active workspaces and page scaffolding.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary document canvas, active text inputs, and focus cards.
- **Secondary Surface (`#EEF2F0`)**: Utility toolbars, sidebar rails, secondary panel backgrounds, table headers, and compare-diff containers.
- **Borders (`#DCE5E1`)**: Crisp architectural perimeter line for panels, input groups, and structural dividing rules.

### Semantic Status Spectrum
Semantic tokens must never be used alone to communicate state; every status color must be accompanied by persistent text badges or distinct icons:
- **Success (`#15803D`)**: Human-authored verification, valid syntax, saved states.
- **Warning (`#B45309`)**: Uncertain patterns, cautionary verification alerts, credit warnings.
- **Danger (`#B42318`)**: High algorithmic density flags, destructive triggers, quota depletion.
- **Information (`#2563EB`)**: System guidance, informative notes, workspace tips.
- **Mixed Result (`#6D5BD0`)**: Distinct deep iris reserved exclusively for mixed-origin detection patterns, clearly differentiated from binary uncertainty.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear boundaries between editorial content, analytical commentary, and technical precision.

### Type Roles
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used strictly for page titles, panel headings, hero statements, and section divisions. Set with tight tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.025em`) and deliberate weights (600 and 700) to project authority and quiet elegance.
- **Body & UI (Inter)**: The primary vehicle for editorial text, comparison readers, system instructions, and interactive controls. Standard body text is anchored at `16px` with an expansive `26px` line height to ensure fatigue-free reading during comprehensive document reviews.
- **Technical Counters & Data (JetBrains Mono)**: Applied to credit quotas, grapheme cluster counters, character/word counters, sentence indexes, and comparison diff tags. This maintains tabular alignment across real-time dynamic updates.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Paragraphs in writing spaces must cap at a maximum measure of `65ch` to preserve optical cadence.
- Do not apply artificial letter spacing to Inter body copy; let the natural font metrics govern legibility.
- Headline capitalization adheres to standard sentence casing (except brand names), avoiding uppercase shout formatting except for strict status pill tags (e.g., `HUMAN`, `AI`, `MIXED`, `UNCERTAIN`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is disciplined, structured around an architectural grid with clear constraints designed for reading and writing ergonomics.

### Canvas & Shell Dimensions
- **Desktop Application Shell**: Fixed left primary sidebar at `240px` (or `256px` in admin mode), top global navigation bar locked at `64px`, and an active content viewport capped at `1440px`.
- **Public & Landing Shell**: Centered single-canvas layout capped at `1280px` maximum width with fluid side gutters.
- **Base Modular Rhythm**: Root spacing derived from a strict `4px` base increment. Layout gaps adhere to standard spacing tokens (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: Dual-pane split-screen configurations for original-versus-humanized text or input-versus-analysis inspection. Sidebars remain docked.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: Sidebars collapse to responsive overlay drawers. Two-pane text comparisons provide a toggle switch (`Asli` / `Hasil`) or stacked side-by-side mode depending on active orientation.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Native bottom navigation bar (height: `64px`) providing primary pivots (`Beranda`, `Detector`, `Humanizer`, `Akun`). Side-by-side workspace comparison automatically stacks vertically into segmented tabbed views to eliminate horizontal scrolling.

### Touch Target Mandate
All interactive triggers, icon wrappers, select fields, and buttons enforce a strict minimum clickable height and width of `44px` across both touch and pointer devices.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an editorial, publication-like atmosphere, this system rejects high-radius drop shadows, neon underglows, and pseudo-3D layers. Depth is constructed purely through **tonal stratification** and **subtle architectural containment**.

### Surface Elevation Layers
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F7F8F7` establishes the lowest plane, holding structural gutters, page framing, and backdrop whitespace.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Workspaces)**: `#FFFFFF` panels seated directly on Level 0, bordered by a single crisp 1px rule (`#DCE5E1`).
- **Level 2 (Recessed Utility Areas)**: `#EEF2F0` inset containers for toolbar rows, character counting ribbons, and diff highlight backgrounds.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs)**: `#FFFFFF` floating modals and active dropdown menus. Elevated with an ambient, deeply diffused, desaturated shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(23, 35, 31, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 35, 31, 0.04)`.

### Border Discipline
Crisp 1px borders using `#DCE5E1` are the primary visual separator between distinct workspace zones. When an interactive panel or text input gains focus, the perimeter shifts cleanly to `#0F766E` with a concurrent `1px` outer halo using `#DDF3EF`, maintaining precise visual integrity without layout shifting.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated dual-radius hierarchy that balances modern software approachability with the structured crispness of editorial printing.

### Radius Hierarchy
- **Panels & Major Cards (`14px`)**: Applied to primary workspace containers, detection results boards, upload areas, and modal dialog shells. This generous curve softens large workspace blocks while preserving defined boundaries.
- **Inputs, Buttons, & Interactive Controls (`10px`)**: Applied to form fields, primary/secondary action triggers, select boxes, and alert notices. It ensures ergonomic tactile affordance without turning elements into excessive pills.
- **Status Tags & Semantic Pills (`6px` to `8px`)**: Compact indicators and classification labels (`HUMAN`, `AI`, `MIXED`, `UNCERTAIN`) receive tighter cornering to maintain legibility and graphic firmness.
- **Avatar & Icon Badges (`9999px`)**: Reserved solely for user profile avatars and circular counter dots. Main buttons and action chips must never use full pill rounding.

## Components

### Buttons
All buttons maintain a strict minimum height of `44px` with horizontal padding of `20px` (`space-md` + `space-xs`), using `10px` border radius and `Inter 14px/500` or `16px/500`.
- **Primary**: Solid background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state: `#134E4A`. Active: `#0D625C`. Focus: 2px offset with ring `#0F766E`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#DCE5E1`, text `#17231F`. Hover state: background `#EEF2F0`, border `#0F766E`.
- **Subtle / Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#66736E`. Hover state: background `#EEF2F0`, text `#17231F`.
- **Destructive**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#B42318`, text `#B42318`. Hover state: background `#FEE4E2`.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Built for long-form composition. Standard text inputs are `44px` tall; multi-line editor canvases scale fluidly with a minimum height of `320px`.
- Border: 1px `#DCE5E1`, background: `#FFFFFF`, text: `#17231F`. Placeholder text: `#66736E`.
- Focus state: border `#0F766E`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px #DDF3EF`.
- Bottom ribbon: Houses monospace character counters (`JetBrains Mono 12px`) and real-time credit estimators formatted as subtle metadata text.

### Status Badges & Result Cards
The AI Text Detector displays results through one of four categorical states—never numerical probabilities or authorship percentages:
- **HUMAN**: Badge background `#DEF7EC`, text `#15803D`, border 1px `#BCF0DA`. Accompanied by checkmark icon.
- **AI**: Badge background `#FDE8E8`, text `#B42318`, border 1px `#FBD5D5`. Accompanied by analytical marker icon.
- **MIXED**: Badge background `#EDE9FE`, text `#6D5BD0`, border 1px `#DDD6FE`. Indicates composite human-machine structures.
- **UNCERTAIN**: Badge background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border 1px `#FDE68A`. Accompanied by question-circle icon.
- **Mandatory Disclaimer Card**: Displayed in proximity to results on `#EEF2F0` with border `#DCE5E1`: *"Hasil ini menunjukkan pola teks, bukan bukti pasti siapa penulisnya."*

### Workspace Cards & Panels
- Main containers utilize a `#FFFFFF` fill with `14px` border-radius and 1px `#DCE5E1` border.
- Split-screen panels in the Humanizer align flush with minimal `16px` gutters, providing independent scrolling and synced line heights.
- Header bars within panels are fixed at `48px` height on `#EEF2F0` with integrated style profile dropdowns and copy/download utility groups.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Minimum 20px box dimensions placed inside a minimum `44px` touch container.
- Selected state fills with `#0F766E`, border `#0F766E`, checkmark icon in pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus ring: `3px` solid `#DDF3EF`.

### Navigation Elements
- **Desktop Sidebar Navigation Item**: 40px height, 8px radius, text `#66736E`, hover background `#EEF2F0`, active state background `#DDF3EF` with text `#0F766E` and semi-bold weight.
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar**: Fixed bottom pinned bar, height `64px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border-top 1px `#DCE5E1`. Four equally divided touch areas with 20px icons and 11px micro-labels.
