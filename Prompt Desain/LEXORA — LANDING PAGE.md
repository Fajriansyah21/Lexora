# LEXORA — LANDING PAGE

Create a responsive public landing page for Lexora using the established Calm Editorial Intelligence design system.

### Header
- Lexora wordmark on the left.
- Navigation: Produk, Cara Kerja, Harga, Bantuan.
- Login link.
- Primary CTA: Mulai Sekarang.

### Hero Section

Headline:
"Pahami tulisanmu. Perbaiki dengan kendali."

Supporting text:
"Analisis pola tulisan dengan hati-hati, lalu rapikan teks tanpa kehilangan kendali atas maknanya."

Primary CTA: "Coba AI Detector"

Secondary CTA: "Gunakan Humanizer"

Use a clean editorial layout with strong typography. Avoid unnecessary illustrations.

### Product Introduction

Create two distinct product sections.

AI Text Detector:
- Analyze writing patterns.
- Sentence-level explanations.
- Four possible outcomes.
- Clear explanation of uncertainty.
- No authorship percentage.

AI Humanizer:
- Writing style control.
- Personal writing profiles.
- Original and generated text comparison.
- User-controlled editing.

### How It Works

Show a simple four-step process:

1. Masukkan tulisan.
2. Analisis atau revisi.
3. Periksa hasilnya.
4. Salin atau unduh.

### Privacy and Trust

Communicate:
- User text is not used for model training by default.
- Users retain control over their original text.
- Detector results are not definitive proof of authorship.

### Pricing Preview

Use configurable placeholders. Do not invent prices or credit amounts.

### FAQ and Footer

Include common product questions, help links, privacy, terms, and contact information.

Create desktop and mobile versions.
